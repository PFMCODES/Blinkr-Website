Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Blinkr covers the basics properly instead of piling on extras.

Ad blocker, on from the first launch

Ads and trackers are blocked by default on every site. No extension to find, no setup. You can pause it any time from Privacy settings.

Themes & accents

Dark, light or system, with five accent colors that carry through the whole interface.

UI density

From spacious to compact. Make the whole browser fit how you like to work.

Real download manager

Progress, pause and resume, open in folder. Duplicate downloads are caught for you.

Light on resources

Tuned for low memory use, with a capped JS heap and trimmed GPU caching.

Bookmarks & history

Your data lives in files on your computer, not on someone else's server.

Per-site permissions

Camera, location, notifications: you're asked once and can always allow or deny for good.

Privacy

Private by default, not by checklist.
